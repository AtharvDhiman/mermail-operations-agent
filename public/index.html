Zero-Trust Input Sanitization
Inbound emails are treated strictly as passive data. Content sanitization strips prompt-injection attempts and enforces Base58 and EIP-55 address validation before lookup.
Deficit Calculus & Buffer
Computes exact replenishment requirements with a 25% safety buffer to absorb gas volatility. Validates outflows against single-topup and 24-hour aggregate budget limits.
Dual-Control PayBox Settlement
Disbursements require operator authorization via structured preview. Returns secure console deep links and posts signed transaction receipts back to the alert thread.
